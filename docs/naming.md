## Names of functions and variables
1. Functions of CRUD operations in repository:
   - CreateWhat()
   - ReadWhat()
   - UpdateWhat()
   - DeleteWhat()
2. Functions of CRUD or other in service:
    - SaveWhat()
    - GetWhat()
    - FindWhat()
    - ChangeWhat()
    - RemoveWhat()

## Directories style
 - one word - lowercase
 - two or more - snake_case
